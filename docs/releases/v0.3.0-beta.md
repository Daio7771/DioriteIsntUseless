# Diorite Isn't Useless 0.3.0-beta

Someone used to work these mines. Now you can find out where.

![The mouth of an abandoned mine](https://github.com/Daio7771/DioriteIsntUseless/blob/main/docs/images/0.3.0-beta/mine_mouth.png?raw=true)

## What's new

### The abandoned diorite mine
A big one-floor maze buried somewhere between Y 0 and 40, different in every world. A small timber mouth on the surface hides a long ladder down. At the bottom: broken rails, old beams, dead ends, and signs that someone left for you. Somewhere in the middle there's a room that someone has been looking after. It's the only lit place down there.

There's a chest, too. Bring torches.

Mines are rare. You won't need luck to find one, though. Someone knows where they are.

### The Miner's new routine
- **He comes in the morning.** Do his job, sleep in a bed under the open sky, and he'll be there when you wake up. If the job isn't done, or you sleep in a cave, he won't come.
- **Jobs come on paper.** Right-click it to see how far along you are and what it pays. When he pays you, he takes the old paper back and gives you the next one.
- **He talks more and writes less.** His instructions book is the only book you'll get from him. Everything else, he tells you himself.

## Changes
- The note in the top-left corner is gone. The job paper replaces it.
- The Miner no longer comes on a timer.

## Good to know
- Mines only appear in newly generated chunks. In an old world, travel to unexplored land.
- Worlds from 0.2 carry over. Your current job stays, and you'll get its paper on his next visit.
- Still Minecraft **1.20.1** (Fabric). The 1.21.1 port is planned for beta 4.

## Known issues
- In some mines water from nearby underground lakes can leak into a corridor.

## Requirements
- Minecraft 1.20.1, Fabric Loader, Fabric API, Fabric Language Kotlin
- Optional: Cloth Config + Mod Menu (config screen)

Abuse mode is still on by default. There are no flashing lights. But there may be moments that make you jump.
